We believe every child deserves a bright future

Passionate Kids Connect is a non-profit organization based in Moshi, Tanzania. We partner with families, schools, and the community to provide access to education, health support, mentorship, and safe shelter for vulnerable children and orphans.

Focus Areas: Education • Health • Mentorship • Shelter

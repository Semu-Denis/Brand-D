What we do

Our Programs

Projects that bring hope, education, health, and opportunity.

Education Access

We provide scholarships, school supplies, uniforms, and tutoring support to ensure children can attend and succeed in school.

  • Scholarships and fees support
  • Homework clubs and tutoring
  • School supplies and uniforms

Health & Nutrition

We partner with clinics to offer checkups, nutrition, and hygiene education to keep children healthy and thriving.

  • Regular checkups and referrals
  • Nutritious meals and food baskets
  • Hygiene and sanitation education

Mentorship

Through youth clubs and one-on-one mentoring, children learn life skills, leadership, and confidence.

  • Life skills and leadership clubs
  • Career days and role models
  • Psychosocial support

Safe Shelter

We coordinate emergency housing, family reunification, and safe spaces with trusted local partners.

  • Emergency shelter and care
  • Family tracing and reunification
  • Safe spaces and protection

Who we are

About Passionate Kids Connect

We exist to bring hope, education, care, and community support to children and orphans in Moshi, Tanzania.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable children through access to education, health support, mentorship, and safe shelter—so every child can thrive.

Our Vision

A community where every child in Moshi grows up healthy, educated, and equipped to pursue their dreams.

Our Values

Compassion, dignity, accountability, and collaboration—guided by the strength and beauty of Tanzanian culture.

Our Story

Founded by local educators and community leaders, Passionate Kids Connect began with a simple idea: when a child is supported, the whole community is strengthened. Today, we partner with schools, clinics, and volunteers to deliver practical support to the children who need it most.
